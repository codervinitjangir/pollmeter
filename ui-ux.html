FAQ

Quick answers

Can't find what you need? Ask your faculty mentor or contact support below.

Do I need to install an app to join?

No. The quiz runs directly in your mobile browser. Open the page, enter the PIN and you are in.

Where do I find the 6-digit PIN?

Your teacher displays it on the classroom projector screen when the session is open.

Who can sign in to the Faculty Portal?

Faculty and administrators with an official @medhaviskillsuniversity.edu.in account, using Google SSO or an Email OTP Code.

What is the difference between Google SSO and Email OTP?

Google SSO signs you in with one click using your university Google account. Email OTP sends a one-time code to your university email instead.

Can other faculty see my quizzes and gradebooks?

Mentors can only access their own quizzes, assigned batches and gradebooks. Cross-mentor access is blocked on the server.

Are results kept after the session ends?

Yes. Results are stored so faculty can review analytics and export the gradebook, and students can follow their own score history.
